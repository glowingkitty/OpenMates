## Overview

OpenMates focused on AI processing reliability and internal orchestration efficiency. Key updates include refinements to the Health app's appointment logic, enhanced CLI output and artifact handling, and the retirement of legacy OpenCode tooling. Development also continued on the unreleased Tasks platform, specifically regarding Codex integration and encrypted editing.
