# Listening cues during unfinished speech

Decision, 2026-10-26: the user requires silence while speaking or thinking,
without unsolicited “mm”, “mhm”, “yeah”, or other listening cues.

An unfinished or uncertain utterance remains pending silently. No listening-cue
assets, runtime generation, or disabled scaffolding are added. A direct question
such as “Are you still listening?” is a complete request and receives a normal
brief reply through the usual pipeline.
