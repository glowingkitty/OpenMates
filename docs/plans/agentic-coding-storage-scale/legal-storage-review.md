# Storage legal copy review

This is a private review of the session2f80 candidate, not a publication record. The public legal documents are assembled in `frontend/packages/ui/src/legal/buildLegalContent.ts` from `src/i18n/sources/legal/privacy.yml` and `terms.yml`. `shared/docs/privacy_policy.yml` is the canonical privacy mirror. The candidate dates both rendered legal documents **2026-10-03**.

| Section | Earlier wording or omission | Candidate wording now rendered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Privacy quick answers, overview, and encryption promise | Durable storage, backups, and account-key erasure were described too broadly. Settings and memories could appear to be archived in S3; shared content could appear erased by one member's deletion. | Structured chat data is client encrypted before sync; uploaded files and recordings follow a TLS-to-server encryption path. PostgreSQL holds durable encrypted records; regional S3 may hold older chat and artifact ciphertext. A separate Vault-encrypted AI context cache remains server readable for requested processing. Key destruction affects ciphertext protected only by the deleted account's keys; another authorized owner or Team may retain shared content. |
| Privacy retention and erasure | A **user-retrievable 60-day export-backup** assertion lacked verified product evidence. Deletion wording implied immediate physical purge or universal erasure. | The unsupported 60-day export-backup bullet is absent from the rendered policy and canonical mirror. Authorized deletion hides/removes records within its authority and schedules unshared regional objects for verified purge; encrypted copies may remain during that process. Financial, audit, and provider-held records have separate stated bases. Under the currently rendered v1 wording, assistant responses sealed for offline recovery are available in PostgreSQL for up to seven days after save, unless their chat or account is deleted sooner; the pending copy can expire if the client does not return and save it. |

## Staged wording and release gate

`recovery_retention_after_cutover.answer` in `privacy.yml` is staged and unrendered. It describes the v2 rule that a pending sealed output survives until canonical client-encrypted save acknowledgement or authorized deletion, regardless of a retry lease or working-cache expiry; large sealed output may reside in regional S3. It must replace the rendered seven-day v1 wording at the same release boundary as the changed retention behavior. The materialized new API already preserves pending sealed output beyond the old seven-day `expires_at` despite retaining recovery `protocol_version: 1`; that protocol number and the timestamp are not evidence that seven-day expiry still applies. **Do not ship the seven-day copy alongside that new API candidate.** Conversely, do not publish the acknowledgement-based copy against the older expiry runtime. This copy swap is independent of the logical-storage billing flag. `credits.storage_billing_enabled` and `credits.storage_nonpayment_four_warning` exist in `terms.yml` but **are not selected by the legal renderer**. They describe expanded logical storage categories and four successfully delivered, dated warnings. The `storage-billing-failed-{1,2,3,4}.mjml` templates and `email/main.yml` notice keys are staged with `storage_gb`, `credits_needed`, `outstanding_credits`, `deadline_date`, `credits_url`, and `export_url`. Each notice calls its date an earliest possible removal date; a later notice may extend it. These templates require the coordinated durable delivery ledger, final payment and ownership checks, and billing policy activation. Do not publish the staged terms paragraph or send the staged notices against the older warning workflow. Team charging and unpaid Team deletion are not asserted here.

## Evidence and limits

Local checks passed: translation generation for 21 locales, three rendered legal-copy tests, and 21 notice Jinja/translation rendering tests. The notice test does not run full MJML-to-HTML delivery because local `cssutils`/`mjml` dependencies are absent. The storage settings component compiles without Svelte warnings; its new isolated Playwright preview spec has been added but has **not** run in CI. The seven-day v1 retention statement is based on source inspection (`JOB_TTL_MS`, read expiry and cleanup of AVAILABLE/LEASED jobs in the prior `chat-recovery-transaction` operations), not an observation of production configuration. The prepared v2 source retains pending sealed jobs despite that old timestamp and does not time-delete pending typed output in cleanup. No live billing, regional purge, legal-counsel review, deployment, or publication is proved by this note. Candidate source `9e6aa906580ad1af66a914e2c353738964558246` excluded these in-progress billing/legal changes.
