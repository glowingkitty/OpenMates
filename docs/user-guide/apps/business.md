The brand naming focus mode helps develop a brief and compare creative suggestions. A suggested name does not establish trademark or domain availability.

## Company ownership

Ask who owns or runs a company, or which companies a person holds or directs, in Germany, the United Kingdom or France. OpenMates looks the company up in that country's official register: OpenRegister for the German commercial register, Companies House for the UK and the State company directory (Annuaire des Entreprises) for France. Each lookup uses only its own country's register; other countries are reported as not covered.

What you get depends on the register. Germany lists shareholders with their shares, holdings and managing directors. The UK lists people with significant control in ownership bands (for example 25-50 %), officers and a person's appointments. France lists directors only, because shareholders are not published there. Birth dates, private addresses and nationality are never shown. When several companies match, you are asked which one you mean. Identical lookups are reused for 7 days.

## Investor search and fundraising

Investor search finds business angels, angel networks and early-stage investors by topic and region. It runs only on servers where the operator installed investor sources; elsewhere it says it is unavailable. A source that covers only some countries is used only when you ask for one of them, and the result names sources that were skipped. Results are the investors' own public descriptions, not recommendations.

The fundraising focus modes help you find business angels, research one investor, write an outreach message, prepare your pitch and plan a funding round. They do not give investment, legal or tax advice.
